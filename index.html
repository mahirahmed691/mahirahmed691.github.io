Fragrance DNA Matcher

Enter a fragrance (especially popular clone/dupe releases) to identify a higher-end original it is widely considered to be copying or heavily inspired by. This is a curated knowledge-base matcher (not an AI web-scraper) and can be extended with your own entries.

Shortcuts: Ctrl + Enter matches, and Ctrl + S saves.

What this app does (and does not) do
  • Does: match against a curated dupe→original library and explain shared “DNA” (accord structure).
  • Does not: scrape the web or guarantee “official” inspiration claims.
  • Best use: you extend the library with your own entries over time.
